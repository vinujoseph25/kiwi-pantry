Our story

A little store with a big green heart

Kiwi Pantry began — in our imaginations — as a corner dairy in Te Puke that refused to stop stocking the good stuff: sun-gold kiwifruit, proper farmhouse butter, and feijoa everything when the season hits.

Today it's a (fictional) online grocer with one simple promise: fresh kai, fair prices, zero fuss. No subscriptions, no surge pricing, no forty-minute scroll to find the butter.

The honest bit: this store doesn't exist. It's a university web-analytics assignment — every page, product and price is pretend, and the real product being studied is you (anonymously, via GA4 events).

What we stand for

Freshness first

Produce picked ripe and priced to move — because kai should taste like the season it grew in.

Fair prices

Everyday low prices in good old NZD — the yellow sticker is a point of pride, not a clearance bin.

An honest demo

Built to teach GA4 e-commerce tracking. Every event is labelled, inspectable and completely harmless.

For the markers

This site fires all nine GA4 recommended e-commerce events plus share. The full measurement plan, UTM links and deployment steps live in the project's README.md.
